哥布林大調查 is an opt-in VALORANT statistics project for private / invite-only friend groups. Everything shown today is synthetic: no real Riot account data is live, no Riot account connection is live, and this page calls no Riot API.

What it is

  • Private or invite-only friend groups review their own VALORANT matches together.
  • Each member opts in individually; joining a group grants no data access.
  • Data collection, group visibility and public derived analytics are separate permissions, all off by default.

Diff Check is not

  • a Riot rank, MMR, Elo or official ladder
  • opponent scouting or a lookup of players outside your group
  • real-time tactical guidance
